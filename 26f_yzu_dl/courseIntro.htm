  • Grading Policy
    • Track 1
      • 課堂練習：45%，以所有練習之平均計分，預計共 10 次。
      • Practice: 45%, based on the average score of all practices, expected to be 10 in total.
      • 內部競賽：30%，以小規模題目（包含但不限於 Kaggle 遊樂場系列）進行課程內部競賽，預計 3 次，每次 10%。
      • In-Class Competition: 30%, based on small-scale problems (including but not limited to Kaggle Playground series), expected to be 3 competitions, 10% each.
      • 期末專案：25%，以較大規模之題目評分，細節另訂。
      • Final Project: 25%, based on a larger-scale problem, with details to be announced separately.
    • Track 2
      • 內部競賽：30%，以小規模題目（包含但不限於 Kaggle 遊樂場系列）進行課程內部競賽，預計 3 次，每次 10%。
      • In-Class Competition: 30%, based on small-scale problems (including but not limited to Kaggle Playground series), expected to be 3 competitions, 10% each.
      • 期末專案：70%，以較大規模之題目評分，細節另訂。
      • Final Project: 70%, based on a larger-scale problem, with details to be announced separately.
    • 期末成績為 Track 1 與 Track 2 之較大者，期中評量以第一次內部競賽之成績登錄。
    • The final grade is the higher of Track 1 and Track 2. The midterm grade will be recorded using the score from the first in-class competition.
    • 評分標準將有一大部分是根據教師的主觀排名來給分，對於相關部份，僅在同學之繳送內容合於基本規定時保證及格，若不能接受者請慎重考慮是否修課。
    • A significant portion of the grading will be based on the instructor's subjective ranking. For this portion, a passing grade is only guaranteed when your submission meets the basic requirements. If you cannot accept this, please carefully reconsider whether to take this course.
    • 若練習次數等方面有顯著更動時，教師可能會提供選項給同學，來投票決定是否修改配分比率。
    • If there is a significant change in the number of practices or similar factors, the instructor may offer options for students to vote on whether to adjust the grading weights.
