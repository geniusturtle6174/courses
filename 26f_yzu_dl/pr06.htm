Problem Desc

每題 34 分，最高給至 100 分。請都將實驗結果，以註解的方式，在各題的程式碼中提供。

Each question is worth 34 points, up to a maximum of 100 points. Please provide your experiment results as comments in the code of each question.

  • 修改 LSTM 教材中的 MNIST 範例，改以雙（或以上）層雙向的方式來進行。你不必做非常仔細的實驗來調整超參數，但至少要讓模型跑出的準確度，不會比範例的單層單向明顯的低。
  • Modify the MNIST example in the LSTM course material to use two (or more) bidirectional layers. You do not need to run very careful experiments to tune the hyperparameters, but at least make sure the accuracy of your model is not noticeably lower than that of the single-layer unidirectional example.
  • 重複上一題，但是改為修改 Transformer 教材的 MNIST 範例，並使用雙（或以上）層的 encoder layer。
  • Repeat the previous question, but modify the MNIST example in the Transformer course material instead, and use two (or more) encoder layers.
  • 將 Transformer 教材的 MNIST 範例（建議使用原始版，而不是上一題的修改版），改為使用 Fashion-MNIST，並比較有無 PositionalEncoding 的準確度。
  • Change the MNIST example in the Transformer course material (using the original version is recommended, not the modified version from the previous question) to use Fashion-MNIST, and compare the accuracy with and without PositionalEncoding.
