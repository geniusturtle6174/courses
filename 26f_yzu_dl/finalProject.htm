Final Proj.

(Updated on 09/18) For those considering whether to pick ICASSP GC, MOE AI CUP, or similar competitions as your final proj. topic

  • 教師會對進度及成果有較高的要求，但若達成時，本專案會有比較高的成績，且對本課程之其他要求會有減免。
  • The instructor will have higher expectations for your progress and results, but if you meet them, you will receive a higher grade on this project, and some of the other requirements for this course will be waived.
  • 承上，尚未開始做專題之大學部同學，若能達成要求，則可將此主題用於次學期由本課程教師指導之專題報告之大部分。
  • Following the above, for undergraduate students who have not yet started their graduation project, if you can meet the requirements, you may use this topic for most of your graduation project report in the following semester, supervised by the instructor of this course.
  • Recommended Topics for ICASSP 2027 GC
    • 運算資源以筆電等級為主者 For students with primarily laptop-level computing resources
      • GC-11: EEG-sAAD 2027: EEG Selective Auditory Attention Decoding
      • GC-13: DOSE-I Challenge: Forecasting Response to Propofol
      • GC-5: FLAG 2027: Face-voice Association across Languages and Gender（使用官方 embedding 時）(when using the official embeddings)
    • 有消費級桌機以上之運算資源者 For students with consumer desktop-level or higher computing resources
      • GC-10: Cadenza Challenge 2027: Predicting Lyric Intelligibility (CLIP2)
      • GC-16: LRAC 2.0: The Second Low-Resource Audio Codec Challenge（僅訓練出合規版，不追求效能時）(when only aiming for a compliant, working version rather than competitive performance)
      • GC-5: FLAG 2027: Face-voice Association across Languages and Gender（試著自己抽取 embedding 時）(when attempting to extract your own embeddings)

Detailed Rules

  • 分組或個人進行，人數：一至四人一組
  • Work may be done individually or in groups of one to four people.
  • 專案內容可於下列限制內自由選擇： Project topics may be freely chosen within the following constraints:
    • 以訓練機器學習或類神經網路模型為核心工作。
    • The core work must involve training a machine learning or neural network model.
    • 預估難度或耗時，至少需為課程內部競賽的三倍以上。
    • The estimated difficulty or workload should be at least three times greater than any in-class competition.
    • 欲選擇 ICASSP 2027 Grand Challenge、教育部 AI CUP，或其他於本學期進行之國內外競賽項目為題目者，應先取得教師同意。
    • If you wish to choose the ICASSP 2027 Grand Challenge, the Ministry of Education's AI CUP, or another domestic or international competition held this semester as your topic, you must obtain the instructor's approval in advance.
    • 本學期開學後尚在進行的現有專案中，符合前列條件者。惟若要將現所屬研究室，或現所修其他課程之題目，用於本專案時，應自行確保指導教授或其他授課教師同意。
    • Existing projects that are still in progress after the start of this semester may be used, provided they meet the above conditions. However, if you plan to use a project from your current research lab or another course you are taking, you must ensure that you have obtained approval from your advisor or the respective course instructor.
  • 期末需繳交書面報告，以及用投影片做口頭報告。 A written report and an oral presentation with slides are required at the end of the semester.
    • 書面報告：以正體中文或英文撰寫，並應使用 ICASSP 2026 template (https://cmsworkshops.com/ICASSP2026/papers/paper_kit.php#Templates)，且建議撰文風格與論文相似。篇幅不受該會議之限制，但不建議超過八頁。
    • Written report: It should be written in Traditional Chinese or English, and must use the ICASSP 2026 template (https://cmsworkshops.com/ICASSP2026/papers/paper_kit.php#Templates). The writing style is recommended to resemble that of an academic paper. The length is not restricted by the conference limits, but it is recommended not to exceed 8 pages.
    • 口頭報告：於第 18 週實體進行，每組推派至少一人參加。其投影片之製作，請把書面報告當作論文，並以「至研討會報告論文」的風格製作。
    • Oral presentation: To be conducted in person in Week 18, with at least one member from each group attending. The slides should be prepared by treating the written report as a paper and following the style of presenting a paper at a conference.
    • 書面報告與口頭報告之檔案，皆需以 pdf 格式於第 18 週繳交。
    • Both the written report and the oral presentation files must be submitted in PDF format in Week 18.

Grading Policy

  • 根據教師的主觀排名來給分。
  • Grading will be based on the instructor's subjective ranking.
  • 原則上，同一組內之所有成員同分。你若認為應有差異時，最遲應於口頭報告當日以 email 提出。
  • In principle, all members within the same group will receive the same score. If you believe there should be a difference, you must raise it by email no later than the day of the oral presentation.
  • 教師將不定期跟各位確認題目選擇或實作進度之情形，並可能會將其納入專案之評分考量。
  • The instructor will periodically check your topic selection or implementation progress during class time, and this may be included as part of the project evaluation.
